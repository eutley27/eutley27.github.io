Weather Forecasting, Price Discovery & Risk Premia in Commodity Futures

Planned · 2027

How do advances in weather forecasting affect price discovery and risk premia in commodity futures markets?

The focus is on regime dynamics, meaning how forecast-driven information shocks move markets between states, and on what that means for systematic trading strategies. I'll build the background for this through three research-heavy courses outside the core MSFM curriculum:

  • Time Series Forecasting: Statistics PhD course
  • Macro Time Series Analysis: Economics
  • Scientific Machine Learning for Climate Physics: Statistics

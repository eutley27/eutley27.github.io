Biography

Ethan Utley is a Master of Science in Financial Mathematics student at the University of Chicago, expected to graduate in December 2027. He is interested in time series, regime dynamics, and how machine learning can be applied to financial markets.

Before UChicago, Ethan spent about five years in quantitative modeling consulting. Before that he spent four years at Protiviti in Risk Analytics, where he developed and independently validated credit and market risk models, including derivatives pricing, PD/LGD, mortgage prepayment, and ML-based fraud detection.

This fall he is working with Manteio Capital through a UChicago Project Lab, exploring transformer models on high-frequency futures data. Outside of work he enjoys prediction markets, real estate and DIY projects, golf, piano, and the NYT crossword.

2026 · Ongoing

Replication: “A Tale of Two Premiums” Ongoing

A replication of A Tale of Two Premiums: The Role of Hedgers and Speculators in Commodity Futures Markets by Wenjin Kang, K. Geert Rouwenhorst, and Ke Tang (Journal of Finance, 2020). The methodology and findings are the authors’. The paper shows that short-term changes in traders’ positions mostly reflect speculators’ demand for liquidity, while long-term positions mostly reflect hedgers’ demand for price insurance, and that the two effects push futures risk premia in opposite directions.

After reproducing the original results, the next step is an attempted extension using data from after the paper’s sample. The question is whether the two premiums still hold through the regime shifts since 2020, such as the COVID-era dislocations, the 2022 energy and inflation shock, and the move to higher interest rates.
